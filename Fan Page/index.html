My Love of Fall

I love fall because it's the perfect time to enjoy a cool, brisk breeze and cozy up with a hot beverage, candles and the perfect movie, tv show or book.

I especially love watching the leaves turn into beautiful golden yellows and rusty oranges, like a warm, multi-colored blanket stretching as far as the eye can see.

These are the top 3 places I want to visit to see autumn in all its glory:

  • Stowe, Vermont
  • Leavenworth, WA
  • Whitefish, Montana
