About Me

Hafsa Siddiqi is a multilingual communications leader with over a decade of experience shaping strategic PR and media relations across the lifestyle, hospitality and higher education sectors. She currently serves as Senior Manager of Media Relations at the University of Maryland and previously held a senior executive role at MoKi Media, a boutique public relations agency in Washington, D.C.


She is known for elevating brand visibility and driving audience engagement through integrated, multimedia campaigns at both regional and national levels. Hafsa brings a culturally informed lens to storytelling, crafting narratives that resonate across diverse audiences and connect with purpose.


Beyond her communications work, Hafsa is the founder and creative director of Hafuusa Photography, a visual brand exploring luxury travel, fashion, and lifestyle through a global, cross-cultural lens. Whether behind the scenes or behind the camera, she thrives at the intersection of creativity, insight and cultural fluency.
